# Specification

## Goal

Define testable product behavior and measurable outcomes without prematurely choosing an implementation.

## Context

Describe the problem, affected users, current behavior, research, and business or technical motivation.

## Constraints

- Applicable project principle
- Compatibility, security, privacy, performance, or compliance constraint
- Delivery boundary

## Principles

List non-negotiable rules and explain how they affect this change.

## Acceptance scenarios

- `AC-001`: Given [state], when [action], then [observable result].
- `AC-002`: Given [failure or edge state], when [action], then [observable result].

## Functional requirements

- `REQ-001`: The system must provide a testable behavior.
- `REQ-002`: The system must preserve a named behavior.

## Non-functional requirements

- `NFR-001`: Measurable performance, reliability, security, or accessibility target.

## Key entities and terms

Define important domain terms and relationships.

## Edge cases and error behavior

- Edge condition and expected outcome

## Assumptions

- Assumption and validation owner

## Non-goals

- Explicitly excluded behavior

## Success criteria

- `SC-001`: Measurable, implementation-independent outcome

## Open questions

- [ ] Material ambiguity that blocks approval

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Stories are prioritized and independently testable.
- [ ] Requirements and scenarios use stable IDs.
- [ ] Edge cases, assumptions, non-goals, and measurable outcomes are explicit.
- [ ] The specification is complete, consistent, testable, and approved.

## Work checklist

- [ ] Identify applicable principles.
- [ ] Validate user stories and priorities.
- [ ] Write requirements and acceptance scenarios.
- [ ] Resolve material ambiguities.
- [ ] Review for consistency and testability.
- [ ] Record approval and hand off to planning.

## Verification

Describe how reviewers checked completeness, consistency, testability, and independence.

## Evidence

Link research, user decisions, current behavior, prototypes, and approval.

## Completion report

Summarize approved stories, requirements, exclusions, and unresolved non-blocking assumptions.

## Follow-ups

List deferred requirements or research that does not block the approved scope.

# Architecture proposal

## Goal

Obtain explicit approval for a design that satisfies the clarified feature outcome.

## Context

Link the completed discovery task, user decisions, and relevant architecture guidance.

## Constraints

- Invariant or public contract
- Compatibility or migration requirement
- Performance, security, privacy, or dependency limit

## Proposed design

Describe components, interfaces, data and control flow, persistence, error handling, and user-visible behavior.

## Affected seams

- File, module, interface, or service and its responsibility

## Alternatives considered

### Option A

Benefits, costs, risks, and reason accepted or rejected.

### Option B

Benefits, costs, risks, and reason accepted or rejected.

## Migration and rollout

Describe compatibility, data migration, feature flags, rollback, and deployment order when applicable.

## Verification strategy

Identify unit, integration, end-to-end, manual, performance, and migration proof needed.

## Risks and mitigations

- Risk, trigger, mitigation, and owner

## Approval

- Status: Pending
- Approver:
- Decision and requested changes:

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The design covers clarified behavior and failure cases.
- [ ] Alternatives and material tradeoffs are visible.
- [ ] Repository invariants and constraints are satisfied.
- [ ] Verification and migration are credible.
- [ ] Approval is explicit before implementation.

## Work checklist

- [ ] Convert discovery into a concrete design.
- [ ] Check affected callers and interfaces.
- [ ] Evaluate alternatives and risks.
- [ ] Define implementation slices and dependencies.
- [ ] Present the proposal and record approval.

## Verification

Record design reviews, prototypes, dependency checks, or technical experiments used to validate feasibility.

## Evidence

Link discovery references, diagrams, experiment results, and approval.

## Completion report

Summarize the approved design, decisive tradeoffs, migration, and planned slices.

## Follow-ups

List optional improvements or deferred risks outside the approved design.

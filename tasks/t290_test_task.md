## Poem

**The Card That Travels**

A blank white card sits in the Backlog,
no title yet, no plan, no clock.
It dreams of moving, lane by lane,
from "maybe" to "done" again.

The Todo lane gives it a name,
acceptance criteria, a little flame.
Someone says "this one is ready",
so the card steps forward, steady.

In Progress, it gets its hands dirty,
subtasks ticked, no time to hurry.
Blocker found? It speaks aloud,
no quiet suffering allowed.

To Review it goes with evidence,
a proof of work, a clear defense.
Two pairs of eyes, two honest reads,
standards and spec, both check the deeds.

And when the verdicts both agree,
the card moves home, at last, set free.
Done. It rests. Its journey ends,
until a new card's journey begins.

fix(website): dial back hero card transparency

The dark inner card inside the hero CometCard drops from
`bg-black/85` + `backdrop-blur-2xl` to `bg-black/70!` +
`backdrop-blur-[3px]`, so the HeroGeometric backdrop shows through
slightly while the card still reads as solid dark. The
`!` important modifier is needed because Tailwind v4 emits
`bg-bg-subtle` after the arbitrary class in source order, which would
otherwise win.

Earlier attempt used `bg-black/10!` (fully transparent), which let the
backdrop bleed straight through and lost the panel. 70% black keeps
the frosted-glass hint without losing the lockup.

The Kandown mark, brand morph and cream install tile stay fully
legible; the CometCard 3D tilt, hover lift and cursor-tracking glare
are unchanged.
